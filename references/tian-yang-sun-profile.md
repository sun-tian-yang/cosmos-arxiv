# Tian-Yang Sun research profile

Use this as the default relevance map. Verify details from arXiv/INSPIRE when accuracy matters.

## Main themes

- Gravitational-wave parameter inference with deep learning, neural posterior estimation, normalizing flows, and robustness to glitches/transient noise.
- Space-based GW inference, including Taiji-related compact-binary inference.
- Simulation-based / likelihood-free inference for 21-cm forest observations and other non-Gaussian cosmological signals.
- CMB power spectra, neural compression/autoencoding, and anomaly or parameter inference with deep learning.
- GW standard sirens and cosmological parameter estimation, including H0 tension and future-detector prospects.
- Dark energy, dynamical dark energy, Quintom-like phenomenology, DESI/BAO/CMB late-universe constraints when linked to standard sirens or model comparison.

## User email style

The user prefers a three-paragraph citation-request email:

1. First paragraph: congratulations plus specific praise for the target paper.
2. Second paragraph: `In parallel, we have been exploring...` or `Recently, we have also worked on...`, followed by exact title(s), arXiv ID(s), and a concise description of the user's related work.
3. Third paragraph: state the shared methodological or scientific theme and politely request citation, typically with `we would be very grateful if you would consider citing our related work(s) in a future revision`.

Close with `We will continue to follow your research with great interest.` when it fits the tone.
